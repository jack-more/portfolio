Panel 05 — Directions
05

Directions For Use

Apply weekly. Do not dilute.
1

Map the demand

Before any spend, we split the demand that already exists from the demand that has to be created. Those are different budgets and different jobs.

2

Match the layer

Creative, offer, and placement are built per layer. A latent-demand ad written like a capture ad fails at both.

3

Measure per layer

Capture on incrementality, latent on lift in branded search and direct, lifecycle on repeat rate. One blended number hides all three.

4

Roll up to the goal

Each layer reports to the business goal it serves, reconciled weekly against the ledger rather than against what the platforms claim.

Warnings

Read before purchase
Caution Last click is not evidence

Most budget pays for demand it didn’t create

Last click hands the credit to whoever was standing closest to the purchase.

Branded search always posts a beautiful CAC, because the person already knew your name. Something else put it there. Optimise on last click and you defund the layer that fills the top — then spend a quarter wondering why capture volume fell off a cliff.

Our work runs from Toyota and Disney at agency scale to a Beverly Hills DTC brand where we hold the entire engine: the ad account, the creative pipeline, the attribution stack, and the number at the end of the week.
