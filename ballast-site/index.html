What is happening in your account right now, and what we do about it.

Last click hands the credit to whoever was standing closest to the purchase. Branded search posts a beautiful CAC because the person already knew your name. Something else put it there.

Optimise on last click and you defund the layer that fills the top, then spend a quarter wondering why capture volume fell off.

In a regulated category the platform also decides whether you get to run at all. Accounts get cut without explanation, learning resets every time you scale, and creative gets rewritten into something that no longer converts. Compliance is not a checklist we run at the end.
