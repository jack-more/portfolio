Brand system,
derived from the mark

Every value below was measured off the logo file itself — the oblique angle, the ellipse ratio, the outline weight, the colour ramp. Nothing here is a preference. It is what the mark already is, written down so everything else can agree with it.

The 23° oblique is the single most distinctive property. It is steep — roughly twice a normal italic — and it is why the mark reads as motion. Nothing else in the system should try to match it; if a second element leans at 23°, the mark stops being the thing that leans.

The Rampamber → orange → warm rim

The ground is not a flat orange. It runs amber at the core, deepens to orange at 74%, then warms again at the rim — which is why it glows. Any orange field in the system uses that ramp, never a flat fill.

Typography

Archivo, one family

Display oblique

Display upright

Section heading

Body copy sits at 500 weight, 17px, 1.62 leading. Long enough to say something, short enough that nobody has to work for it. Never justified, never centred below three lines.

Data · labels · codes · IBM Plex Mono

One family does the work. Archivo at 900 italic rhymes with the mark without competing with it; at 800–900 upright it carries headings; at 500 it reads as body. Plex Mono is the only second voice, and only for data. A light serif was tried and is wrong: it descends from a different century than the mark and makes the logo look like a sticker somebody left on an essay.

04

Geometry

The lozenge and the die-cut
The lozenge — 1.46 : 1

The ellipse from the mark is the only organic shape in the system. It appears at 1.46:1, never circular, never blobbed, always carrying the ramp.

The die-cut
In-marketLatent

Everything is cut out and outlined the way the wordmark is: 2px ink, square corners, white fill. Buttons, tags, panels, images. No shadows, no soft edges, no gradients on anything but the ellipse.

06

Rules

Learned the hard way

Do

  • Let the mark be the loudest thing on any surface it appears on.
  • Use the ramp for orange, never a flat fill.
  • Outline at 2px ink, square corners, white fill.
  • Keep the lozenge at 1.46:1 whenever it is used as a shape.
  • Set data in Plex Mono and everything else in Archivo.
  • Earn motion: it should show a real state changing, or it should not move.

Don't

  • Pair the mark with a light editorial serif.
  • Draw irregular blobs, cells or organic shapes. The lozenge is the only curve.
  • Fill space with texture — halftone fields, noise, dot screens.
  • Build a boxed demo, simulation or chart to explain the offer.
  • Lean anything else at the mark's 23°.
  • Use packaging voice as copy. The mark already says it.
