Solar Source

Swarming energy

A solar source that hugs the letters and gives them warmth.

It’s swarming energy, not a fixed shape. It runs hottest where it meets the letters and lifts to gold at its outer edge. Airbrushed, never flat, and never quite the same twice.

It’s never shown on its own. The letters always sit inside it.

09

Rules

Always

  • Keep the wordmark inside its solar source.
  • Put blue inside, heat around it.
  • Lead with the number.
  • Use real objects and real footage from the work.

Never

  • Pull the solar source off the mark or reuse it as a background shape.
  • Flatten the heat into a flat orange panel.
  • Stretch, rotate or recolour the lockup.
  • Explain the joke.
