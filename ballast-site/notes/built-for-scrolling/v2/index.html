Built forscrolling

The feed can get anyone to watch anything. Getting them to care was never its job.

Ballast Notes №2 · Jack Morello · November 2026 Scroll ↓
01 · The hook

TikTok’s own people measured how long it takes to hook us. Two hundred and sixty videos, and we’re in.

Internal documents, Kentucky v. TikTok · NPR

That’s about 35 minutes, less than an episode of a sitcom. Nobody has bothered to measure how long it takes us to actually care about the person in one.

02 · The feed

Yes, our friends are still in there somewhere. Meta’s own trial exhibit put them at 7% of our time on Instagram, down from 11% two years earlier.

Meta exhibit, FTC v. Meta, 2025 · Source

And it works, if the goal is time. Researchers switched people to a plain chronological feed, and they simply drifted off and spent less of their lives on the app.

Guess et al., Science, 2023 · Source
03 · The loop

Within about 100 videos the feed has us figured out. After that it serves the same five things on rotation, and we keep showing up for it.

4.9M TikTok videos, 347 real users · Source

We can press Dislike all we want. Across 567 million YouTube recommendations, it cut the unwanted ones by 12%.

Mozilla, 567M YouTube recommendations · Source
04 · Music

Music feels it first. Of everyone who followed an artist on TikTok, 26 in 100 went on to listen to more of their music.

MIDiA Research, 2025 · Source

In 2020 a viral post was worth 738 streams to the artist. By 2025 it was 275. We keep coming back to the snippet, and the song gets left behind.

Chartmetric, 2025 · Source
05 · Sports

Our grandparents watched the whole game. Fans under 30 spend three quarters of their sports time on clips, and 38% of Gen Z don’t have a team at all.

L.E.K. Consulting, 2025 · Source
06 · The split

So fandom splits in two: a huge crowd that never leaves the clip, and one in five who go all the way in.

Luminate, 2026 · Source

That one in five spends $113 a month on live music, against $68 for everyone else. They’re what the whole industry is quietly chasing now.

Luminate, 2025 · Source
07 · The fix

The feed is a great place to meet people and a terrible place to keep them. Recruit there, then bring them somewhere the algorithm doesn’t get a vote.

The Eras Tour sold 10.17 million tickets. Weverse fans spend 263 minutes a month in a room the artist owns. Both are places the feed can’t reach.

What you just read

  1. The feed gets your attention fast and almost never your loyalty.
  2. Fandom is splitting: a huge crowd in the clip, a small core going deep.
  3. Recruit on the feed. Keep people somewhere you own. Measure who moves.
Try this weekTake your 10 most-viewed posts from the last 90 days. Count everyone who did something after watching: signed up, followed you somewhere you own, bought, came back. Divide by views. That’s your real conversion from viewer to fan.
