Common questions

Can Ballast run ads for supplements or research-use-only products?
Yes. Ballast writes claims and creative to each platform's policy, avoids human-use and dosing claims for research-use-only products, and keeps ad accounts in good standing. No agency can guarantee platform approvals, but staying live in restricted categories is Ballast's specialty.
Does Ballast work with small or early-stage brands?
Yes. Ballast often starts brands on TikTok with a few thousand dollars a month in ad spend, then adds Meta and more creative as winning ads emerge.
How does Ballast measure results?
Against real orders and revenue, not the numbers ad platforms report. Ballast reconciles platform reporting to the store and judges spend on what it actually created. Read how.
Who writes Ballast's ad creative?
The founder, Jack Morello, is a copywriter as well as a media buyer and writes the concepts, scripts and ad copy Ballast runs.
How much does Ballast cost?
Engagements range from about $3,500 to $100,000 depending on scope. Most clients start with a 90-day pilot, then go month to month. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms.
