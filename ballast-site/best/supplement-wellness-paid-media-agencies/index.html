How to choose

  1. Category experience: named or verifiable work in supplements, telehealth, weight loss or research-use-only products, not only general DTC.
  2. Platform-policy fluency: how claims are reviewed before launch, and what happens when an ad or account is rejected.
  3. Channel fit: strength on TikTok (Ads, Shop, LIVE, creators) as well as Meta. Many shops are Meta- or Google-first.
  4. Creative engine: who writes and produces the ads, how fast they test, and whether there's a creator program.
  5. Measurement honesty: whether reporting is reconciled to real orders and revenue, or stops at platform ROAS.
  6. Size fit: published minimums and how many clients each manager handles.
  7. Commercial terms: retainer, percent of spend or performance fee, contract length, and who owns the ad accounts and creative.

Common questions

Can an agency get supplement or weight-loss ads approved on Meta and TikTok?
Often, if claims follow each platform's health policy: no disease claims, no unrealistic results, and landing pages that match the ad. No agency can guarantee approval. Ask how they review claims before launch and what they do when an ad or account is restricted.
Can research-use-only (RUO) products be advertised on Meta, Google or TikTok?
The platforms restrict these categories heavily. Ads that make human-use or dosing claims get rejected. Brands that advertise do it with strict research-use language and an agency that knows the current policies; expect some ads to be refused regardless.
Do I need LegitScript certification to advertise telehealth?
Google and Meta generally require LegitScript certification for advertisers that promote online pharmacies or prescription drugs. Check each platform's current policy for your exact offer before you launch.
Should a supplement brand start on TikTok or on Meta?
Many early-stage brands start on TikTok, where creator-style ads and TikTok Shop can find buyers cheaply, then add Meta once they have proven creative. Brands with strong customer data and a retention program often scale fastest on Meta.
How much ad spend do I need before hiring an agency?
It depends on the agency. On this list, published minimums range from about $5,000 a month in fees to $80K+ a month in revenue. Several agencies, Ballast included, work with brands spending a few thousand dollars a month on ads.
How are paid media agencies priced?
Most charge a monthly retainer; some charge a percentage of ad spend or add a performance fee. Ad spend is usually paid directly to the platforms. Check contract length and who owns the ad accounts and creative.
How do I know an agency's ROAS is real?
Ask whether they reconcile platform-reported results to actual orders in your store. Platforms often overcount sales, so a good agency reports against revenue you can see in the bank.
