The uwant lesson

The picker chose the clip with the best early click rate and fed it 42% of the campaign's total spend. It converts one click in 77. The next four creatives together spent $2,675 and returned 80 purchases, $33 each. Uwant spent $3,542 and returned 33 at $107. One creative cost the campaign roughly $2,100 against the account average. CTR was not the tell. Clicks per order was.

A correction on my day-one call

On Sep 3 I recommended shifting budget to biggestsale-ethan-8s, which was at $46 CPA on 8 purchases. On the full week it is 2.24× with a $102 AOV, the lowest in the group. The clips that actually held up are everyone-labor-ethan (8.56×), singod_BOTS (6.66×), pulltrig (5.74×) and the clipvault cut (5.11×). Day-one CPA on single digits of purchases is noise. What was not noise was uwant's clicks-per-order, and that call stands.

What worked in the copy

The three best Ethan clips all name the offer in the first line and end on the code. "Everyone" and "biggest sale" are the same script with different openers; one did 8.56× and one did 2.24×. The difference is the first two seconds. Singod's BOTS and HOW clips, which are him talking about the product and how ordering works, beat his CELEBRATE, LUCKY and PRICE clips by 2–3×. Product beats mood.

AI content, again

Five TikTok AI Editor clips and the AI presenter clip: $406, 3 purchases, $135 each. Same finding as August. AI product photography sells (the Higgsfield round is 18× this week on a tiny budget). AI people don't.

Tracking — what changed and what it means for the numbers

  • One container, one property. GTM-TMG5WDJQ is the only container on the site and it loads. GA4 property 543439378 is the only one receiving. The old property (549569344) is abandoned; its data was duplicated ~2.7× and should not be quoted.
  • TikTok receives one Purchase per order. Verified in Events Manager: Sep 4 shows 100 server-and-browser matched purchases against 6 browser-only. Sep 3 showed 198 against 201, the duplicate. The extra tag is paused (container v9).
  • Click IDs land. The ad URL carries ttclid; the store writes it to a cookie and to its own attribution records on the landing session. Verified on /lp with a test click.
  • Phone is now sent on every TikTok event from GTM, alongside email. Expect EMQ to move over the next two weeks.
  • The admin's "TikTok Ads" label undercounts by about half. Any order using an affiliate code is filed under Affiliate even when the pixel matched a TikTok click. When reconciling, use the source filter (pixel-matched), not the label.

The rejection and the landing page

TikTok's reviewer reads the landing page. The homepage carries "GLP-1 / GIP Dual Receptor Agonist" as a category name, "GLP-3" in a product name, "appetite regulation," "adipose tissue," and a "Metabolic Reference Bundle." The disclaimer doesn't matter; the classifier does. A competitor running the same compounds on TikTok has zero of those words anywhere on their site.

Fix in place: skocompounds.com/lp. Clean of every trigger word, 11 non-GLP SKUs, same cart and tracking, and the offer comes from the URL (lp_code, lp_offer) so no sale is ever hard-coded. Evergreen with no parameters. All new ads point here. Tickets for Lovable cover the site-wide word scrub, the product photography on the main site, and the storefront rebuild.
