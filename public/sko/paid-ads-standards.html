Spend control — the Eyepocalypse clause

  1. New concept bets cap at $500 lifetime until the first ≥1× day. No exceptions, no "it needs more data." Eyepocalypse burned $7.9K across two reports after its answer was in at $500.
  2. Kill any ad at $150 spend / 0 purchases.
  3. Graduate at < $60 CPA on 5+ purchases — move to WINNERS at a $30–50/day floor.
  4. No edits to live ads; duplicate. No budget moves inside 72h learning. Raises ≤ 30% per 72h.
  5. Never hard-pause a winner for stock issues — throttle to $20–30/day floors. A paused campaign loses its learning; a throttled one doesn't.
  6. Smart+ creative pools: ≤ 10 creatives. Monthly prune of anything at $50+ spend with zero purchases.
  7. One unique code per campaign, assigned at launch. That is what keeps the codes × attribution report readable.
  8. Creative verdicts issued during a stock-out are void. Re-test after restock.

Measurement standards

  1. Attribution windows: 7-day click / 1-day view everywhere. Day boundary America/Panama on every ads surface.
  2. Weekly export: ad level, daily split, with Adds to Cart.
  3. Monthly audit: verified-floor ratio (20–30% of platform expected; below 15% means click capture is breaking) · MER · checkout completion (below 30% = fix the funnel before buying more traffic).
  4. Budgets are set from platform CPA; the business case is made from the verified floor + MER. Never quote a platform number to leadership without its floor beside it.
