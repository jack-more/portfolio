Where it lives now

The honest picture

Your best nights are on somebody else’s hard drive.

TodayScattered
  • Clips live in re-uploads, dead links, and private drives.
  • No way to search for a specific play, game, or night.
  • Quality degrades with every re-upload.
  • The people preserving it get no credit and no pay.
  • A nineteen-year-old who’d love your game will never find it.
AfterOne place
  • Every clip in one archive, labeled and dated.
  • Searchable down to the possession.
  • Originals kept untouched, permanently.
  • Everyone who worked on it is named on it.
  • New fans find you every week, on purpose.

Who does the work

Not us. Not strangers.

The people who already keep your highlights alive — the ones cutting clips at 2am for no money and no credit. We find them, pay them, and put their name on the work.

ArchivistsFans who find the footage, cut it, and label it properly. Every clip carries the name of who saved it.
KeepersYou appoint five. They curate your archive and decide what belongs. It is an honor, not a job application — and only you hand it out.
EditorsThey build the mixes that travel. Every one credits you and the archivists behind it.
UsWe build the tools and run the operation. We do not own your footage and we never will.

What you control

All of it
01

You own the archive

Your footage, your stories, your likeness. Ours is the workshop, not the vault. If you walk, the archive walks with you — in writing, before anything starts.

02

Nothing goes out unapproved

You or your Keepers clear what gets published. Anything you want down comes down, no conversation required.

03

The footage is never altered

No upscaling, no generated frames, nothing invented. Edits are layers on top; the original stays exactly as it was recorded.

04

Your name, always attached

Every clip, every mix, every credential traces back to you. The archive grows your name by design, not as a side effect.
